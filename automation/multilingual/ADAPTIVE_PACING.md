# Adaptive Pacing Engine

---

## 1. Core Mechanics

Adaptive pacing adjusts spoken speed based on content complexity:
- **Slide Introductions & Recap:** May be accelerated up to `1.08x` (preferred) or `1.12x` (max).
- **Live Code Execution & Syntax Walkthroughs:** Pacing locked at `1.00x`.
- **Architecture Explanations:** Pacing locked at `1.00x` with natural cognitive pauses.
